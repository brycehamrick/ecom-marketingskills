# Non-Meta Paid Social Platforms

Meta is covered in `SKILL.md`. This file covers the platforms that behave differently enough to need their own approach.

The general rule: do not port a Meta account structure to another platform and expect it to work. Each has a different buyer intent, a different creative expectation, and a different measurement reality.

---

## TikTok

**Who it works for:** visual, demonstrable, impulse-friendly products under roughly $100. Beauty, food and beverage, home, gadgets, apparel, pet. Weak for high-consideration and high-AOV purchases.

**What is different:**

- **Creative is even more dominant than on Meta.** A polished brand film reliably underperforms a phone-shot video. Native-feeling content is not a stylistic preference here, it is the requirement
- **Fatigue is much faster.** Concepts burn out in one to three weeks. Production cadence must be higher than for Meta or the account decays
- **Sound is on.** Unlike Meta, most viewing is with audio. Trending audio, voiceover, and on-screen captions all matter
- **Smart Performance Campaign** is the consolidated automated option, analogous to Advantage+
- **Spark Ads** — running an ad from a creator's own handle rather than the brand's. Consistently outperforms brand-handle ads. Requires the creator's authorization code. See `creators-and-affiliates`
- **TikTok Creative Center** is public and shows top-performing ads by category — an unusually good free research input for `category-intel`

**Measurement:** attribution runs shorter and view-through claims are generous. Weight MER and holdout tests heavily rather than platform-reported ROAS.

**Policy:** stricter than Meta on health, wellness, weight, and financial claims. Before/after is effectively unusable in most categories. See `claims-and-compliance`.

**TikTok Shop** is a separate motion — in-app checkout, affiliate creators, and live selling. Treat it as a marketplace channel, not a paid social channel. See `marketplace-listings`.

## Pinterest

**Who it works for:** home, decor, apparel, wedding, food, DIY, beauty, gifting. Strongly female-skewed audience. Poor fit for most B2B-adjacent or utility products.

**What is different:**

- **Intent is higher and slower.** Pinterest is planning-stage rather than impulse. Attribution windows are genuinely longer — a pin can convert months after it is saved
- **Vertical static images still work.** One of the few platforms where a well-made static image competes with video
- **Keyword targeting is real here**, unlike on Meta. Pinterest functions partly as a visual search engine, so keyword and interest targeting carry actual signal
- **Catalog and Shopping ads** work well; a healthy product feed is a prerequisite. See `catalog-and-feeds`
- **Organic and paid compound.** Pins persist and accumulate reach over time, unlike feed content elsewhere

**Structure:** simpler than Meta. Consolidated campaigns, broad plus keyword targeting, catalog-driven Shopping campaigns for the long tail.

**Measurement:** long conversion lags mean short-window reporting understates it. Use a 30-day window at minimum and lean on MER.

---

## Snapchat

**Who it works for:** under-30 audiences, impulse products, apparel, beauty, gaming and accessories. Narrower than TikTok, cheaper CPMs.

**What is different:**

- Vertical full-screen video only; sound-on expectation
- Creative must feel like a Snap, not an ad — the same native requirement as TikTok
- CPMs are typically well below Meta, which makes it a useful diversification channel when Meta CPMs spike in Q4
- Smaller reach means saturation comes faster in narrow categories

**Realistic role:** a secondary channel that adds incremental volume, not a primary acquisition engine for most brands.

---

## Reddit

**Who it works for:** products with a passionate hobbyist or problem-focused community — fitness, skincare, coffee, pets, gaming, tools, supplements, tech.

**What is different:**

- **Subreddit targeting is the whole advantage.** Precise communities with real intent, which is the opposite of the broad-targeting logic that wins on Meta
- **Ads must not read as ads.** Reddit audiences are hostile to obvious marketing. Conversational copy, honest framing, and a willingness to engage in comments
- **Comments are part of the ad.** Leaving them unmoderated on a skeptical audience is worse than not running
- Conversion tracking is less mature; measurement requires more care

**Realistic role:** a strong niche channel for the right category, and a poor general-purpose one. Also valuable as a research surface for `customer-research` regardless of whether ads run.

## Platform Selection

| If the product is… | Prioritize |
|---|---|
| Visual, impulse, under $100 | Meta, TikTok |
| Home, decor, gifting, wedding | Pinterest, Meta |
| Under-30 fashion, beauty, accessories | TikTok, Snapchat |
| Hobbyist or problem-focused niche | Reddit, Meta |
| High-AOV considered purchase | Meta, plus `google-ads` for capture |
| Marketplace-first | `marketplace-ads` before any of these |

**Do not add a platform until the primary one is running well.** A second platform run poorly costs more than it earns, and it splits the creative production capacity that the first platform needs.

---

## Porting Errors to Avoid

- Reusing Meta creative on TikTok without reshooting for the native format
- Applying Meta's broad-targeting doctrine to Reddit, where precision is the point
- Judging Pinterest on a 7-day attribution window
- Expecting Snapchat to scale to Meta volumes in a narrow category
- Running any secondary platform without enough creative supply to feed it
