## Agent Notes

- Useful for the daily/weekly/monthly reporting cadence in `measurement-and-analytics` without building dashboards from scratch.
- **Populate COGS and shipping cost inputs**, or the "profit" figures are revenue with a different label. Contribution margin is the number that matters.
- Cohort retention views support the cohort analysis in `retention-and-loyalty` — segment by acquisition channel and first product, which is the cut that changes decisions.
- **Automate the data pull, write the narrative by hand.** A dashboard link is not a client report. See `client-reporting`.
- **Related skills**: `measurement-and-analytics`, `client-reporting`, `retention-and-loyalty`
