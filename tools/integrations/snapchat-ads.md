## Authentication

- **Type**: OAuth 2.0
- **Base**: `https://adsapi.snapchat.com/v1`

## Agent Notes

- **CPMs are typically well below Meta**, which makes Snapchat a useful diversification channel when Meta CPMs spike in Q4.
- Vertical full-screen video only, sound-on expectation. Creative must feel native — the same requirement as TikTok, and ported Meta creative underperforms.
- Audience skews under 30. Narrow reach means saturation arrives faster in niche categories.
- **Realistic role**: a secondary channel adding incremental volume, not a primary acquisition engine for most brands.
- **Related skills**: `paid-social`, `ad-creative`
