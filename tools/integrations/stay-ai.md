## Agent Notes

- Differentiator is retention tooling: cancel-flow experimentation, churn prediction, and A/B testing of save offers.
- **The cancel flow is the highest-leverage surface in a subscription program.** Stay AI makes it testable — use it to match each stated cancellation reason to the right alternative (skip, pause, swap, smaller size) rather than defaulting to a discount.
- Do not let testing tooling justify an obstructive cancel flow. Making cancellation hard generates chargebacks and negative-option regulatory exposure. See `claims-and-compliance`.
- Churn predictions are a model; validate against actual cohort retention before acting on them at scale.
- **Related skills**: `subscriptions-and-replenishment`, `experimentation`, `retention-and-loyalty`
